# Beacon pilot handover plan

Support will become the first point of contact for the six Beacon pilot customers at 09:00 UTC on 19 October 2026, if readiness is confirmed as described below. Launch has managed the pilot since August, with informal help from Support. Launch remains responsible for unresolved setup defects until 30 October 2026.

## Scope

The handover covers inbound questions, access requests, and coordination of incident updates for only the six customers listed in `beacon-pilot-register-v3`. It excludes onboarding new customers and approving changes to subscription terms. Sales retains responsibility for commercial questions. The register is the definitive list; a customer expressing interest in the pilot does not automatically come into scope.

## Handover materials

- Maya Chen, the Launch lead, will deliver the account notes, known-issue list, and recorded walkthrough by 17:00 UTC on 14 October 2026. Each account note must identify the customer's primary contact and any open commitment with a due date.
- Arun Patel, the Support lead, will review the package and identify missing information by 12:00 UTC on 15 October 2026.
- Maya will resolve those gaps by 16:00 UTC on 16 October 2026.

This check repeats some of the usual account review, but is needed because pilot commitments are currently held in separate notes.

## Readiness and transfer

Two dependencies have the same deadline, 12:00 UTC on 16 October 2026: IT must grant the four assigned Support agents access to the pilot console, and Finance must confirm the escalation contact for billing questions. Arun is responsible for checking that both are complete.

If either remains open at 16:00 UTC on 16 October 2026, Arun will mark the handover "Not ready" in `BEACON-42`, and Launch will continue handling customer requests. The next readiness review will take place at 12:00 UTC on 19 October 2026. Ownership will not transfer automatically at that review.

If readiness is confirmed on 16 October, Arun will add "Ready for transfer" to `BEACON-42`. Maya will then send the six customers the agreed contact instructions.

During the first week after transfer, Support will route setup defects to Launch with the account name, reproduction steps, and customer impact. Support will continue communicating with the customer while Launch investigates.

## Ending Launch's temporary responsibility

Maya and Arun will review open setup defects at 15:00 UTC on 30 October 2026. That review does not automatically end Launch's responsibility. Before that responsibility ends, every remaining defect must have a named engineering owner and a next update date. Arun is responsible for the ongoing support queue after that point. This final review should ensure unfinished work has an owner when the temporary arrangement ends.
