# Beacon pilot handover plan

Support is due to become the first point of contact for six Beacon pilot customers at 09:00 UTC on 19 October 2026, subject to the readiness checks below. Launch has managed the pilot since August, with informal help from Support. Launch will remain responsible for unresolved setup defects until the closure arrangements on 30 October 2026.

The handover covers only the customers in `beacon-pilot-register-v3`. It includes incoming questions, access requests, and coordinating incident updates. It excludes onboarding new customers and approving subscription changes. Sales still handles commercial questions. Interest in joining the pilot does not put a customer on the handover list.

## Preparing the handover

The leads will complete these steps in order:

- By 17:00 UTC on 14 October 2026, Launch lead Maya Chen will deliver account notes, the known-issue list, and a recorded walkthrough. Each account note must name the customer's primary contact and any open commitment with a due date.
- By 12:00 UTC on 15 October 2026, Support lead Arun Patel will review the package and identify missing information.
- By 16:00 UTC on 16 October 2026, Maya will resolve those gaps.

Some of this repeats the usual account review, but pilot commitments are held in separate notes, so the check is needed.

## Confirming readiness

By 12:00 UTC on 16 October 2026, IT must give the four assigned Support agents access to the pilot console, and Finance must confirm the escalation contact for billing questions. Arun will check that both are complete.

If either is still open at 16:00 UTC that day, Arun will mark the handover "Not ready" in `BEACON-42`. Launch will continue handling customer requests. The next readiness review will be at 12:00 UTC on 19 October 2026; that review will not automatically transfer ownership.

If readiness is confirmed on 16 October, Arun will add "Ready for transfer" to `BEACON-42`. Maya will then send the six customers the agreed contact instructions.

## After transfer

During the first week, Support will send setup defects to Launch with the account name, reproduction steps, and customer impact. Support will keep communicating with the customer while Launch investigates.

Maya and Arun will review open setup defects at 15:00 UTC on 30 October 2026. Before Launch's temporary responsibility ends, every remaining defect must have a named engineering owner and a next update date. Arun owns the ongoing support queue after that point. This review closes the temporary arrangement while keeping unfinished work assigned to someone.
