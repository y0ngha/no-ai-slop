# Beacon pilot handover plan

As we move towards the next phase of the Beacon pilot, a clear allocation of responsibilities will be useful to support continuity across the teams involved. The pilot has been managed by the Launch team since August, with Support providing informal help. The practical change in this handover is that Support becomes the first point of contact for the six pilot customers at 09:00 UTC on 19 October 2026. Launch remains responsible for unresolved setup defects until 30 October 2026.

The scope is limited to the six customers listed in `beacon-pilot-register-v3`. It includes inbound questions, access requests, and coordination of incident updates. It does not include onboarding new customers or approving changes to subscription terms. Sales retains ownership of commercial questions. The register is the definitive list for this handover; a customer expressing interest in the pilot does not bring them into scope automatically.

Maya Chen, the Launch lead, will deliver the account notes, known-issue list, and recorded walkthrough by 17:00 UTC on 14 October 2026. Each account note must identify the customer's primary contact and any open commitment with a due date. Arun Patel, the Support lead, will review the package and identify missing information by 12:00 UTC on 15 October 2026. Maya will resolve those gaps by 16:00 UTC on 16 October 2026. There is some repetition with the usual account review process here, but this check is needed because pilot commitments are currently held in separate notes.

The handover depends on two things. IT must grant the four assigned Support agents access to the pilot console by 12:00 UTC on 16 October 2026. Finance must confirm the escalation contact for billing questions by the same deadline. Arun owns checking that both dependencies are complete. If either remains open at 16:00 UTC that day, he will mark the handover "Not ready" in `BEACON-42`, and Launch will continue handling customer requests. The next readiness review will then take place at 12:00 UTC on 19 October 2026; ownership will not transfer automatically at that review.

If readiness is confirmed on 16 October, Arun will add "Ready for transfer" to `BEACON-42`. Maya will then send the six customers the agreed contact instructions. During the first week after transfer, Support will route setup defects to Launch with the account name, reproduction steps, and customer impact. Support will continue communicating with the customer while Launch investigates.

Finally, in relation to closure, Maya and Arun will review open setup defects at 15:00 UTC on 30 October 2026. Any remaining defect must receive a named engineering owner and a next update date before Launch's temporary responsibility ends. Arun owns the ongoing support queue after that point. This final review is intended to close the temporary arrangement without allowing unfinished work to disappear between teams.
