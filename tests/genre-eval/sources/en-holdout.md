Hi team — following the questions about the Marlin export screen, I wanted to provide some clarification around the permissions involved. The confusing part is that seeing a report and being allowed to download its rows are separate permissions. A Viewer can open the report and change its on-screen filters. An Analyst can do those things and export a CSV, but only when the workspace owner has enabled exports. An Owner can enable exports; being an Owner does not itself grant the Analyst permission.

There is also a size restriction. One export can contain at most 5,000 rows, after filters are applied. Exactly 5,000 rows is allowed. If the filtered report contains 5,001 or more, the download is blocked with "Narrow your filters to export 5,000 rows or fewer." The system does not quietly download the first 5,000 rows. That distinction matters because a partial file could look complete to someone using it for a monthly total.
